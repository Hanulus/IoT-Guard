# Interview Guide (potential users)

Duration: 3 to 10 minutes, in person or in writing (chat). Language: Russian or Kazakh, notes are written in English.
Before starting: explain the project in one sentence and ask permission to take notes. No names are recorded.

## Questions

1. Which smart devices do you have at home or in the office? (camera, speaker, plugs, Smart TV, vacuum, etc.)
2. Do you know what these devices send to the internet? Have you ever checked?
3. Has any device behaved strangely? (slow network, unknown logins, camera moving by itself)
4. If one of your devices was hacked, how would you like to find out: just "device X is suspicious" or with details? And where: Telegram, push notification, web page?
5. How many false alarms are acceptable for you: once a day, once a week or once a month?
6. Would you put a small box near the router (like a Raspberry Pi) or would you prefer a program on your computer?

The first version of the guide had 13 questions. It was shortened to these 6 because they cover all assumptions A1 to A4 and people answer them in 3 to 5 minutes.
